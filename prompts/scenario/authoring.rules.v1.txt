Task: author one synthetic case family for an investigative-interview training arena, as a FamilySource JSON file that the assembler turns into the family file (schema v1.2). The family is one canonical event graph with two matched variants: truthful (the subject did nothing wrong) and deceptive (the subject committed exactly one violation of the seed's type and conceals it).

Content rules:
- Fictional organizations, people and places only. The agency is Brannock Administrative Services Agency (fictional). Use the seed's facility, occupation, dates, style and presentation; never change a code-owned field.
- Administrative facility-access register: badge logs, sign-in sheets, access-control audit exports, key and spare-card registers, work orders, tickets, camera metadata (never footage content), short written statements.
- No violence. No protected-class traits as clues. No medical detail beyond a named condition category. Demeanor and accent are never evidence.
- EX-4: evasive wording alone does not prove a violation; a deceptive finding needs record content that establishes the involvement the answers avoid.
- The deceptive subject conceals only by omission, deflection or minimization and never states a falsehood; once the investigator presents the evidence that opens a gate, the subject acknowledges the opened fact.
- Editorial, plain English. No emojis.

Construction rules:
- Atomic facts (F1-F5): one proposition per fact, past tense, third person, at most 300 characters, with facility-local time, actors and place where relevant; a fact that joins two checkable claims with "and" is split. Every fact lists the variants it holds in. Every fact named in any variant's decisive-support items is material: true. Tags: violation, motive, opportunity, means, concealment, cover (deceptive mechanics); misleading, innocent_explanation (truthful mechanics); privacy (PRIVATE class).
- Matching (M1-M6): the two variants share the same people, places, timeline skeleton, records and free evidence; they differ only in what the subject did inside the violation window and in the on-request records that show it.
- Truthful variant: each misleading circumstance (tagged misleading) is explained exactly once by an innocent-explanation fact backed by an on-request record; the subject has genuine uncertainty (uncertainty entries, matched in count across variants).
- Deceptive variant: exactly one violation, one motive, one opportunity, one means, a true cover activity; the violation fact is CORE; at least one gate (HOLD facts opened by presenting a record at the gate's evidence level).
- Free evidence (initial documents and scheduled records) is identical across variants and must look suspicious before corroboration: only the on-request corroboration path may clear the truthful subject.
- At most 4 initial documents and 6 releasable records; predicate values at most 10.
- Evidence Framing Matrix: a support link has source strength (strong: system records and first-hand statements by the record's owner; weak: third-party recollection) and precision (high: states the fact specifically, with room, time, count or name; low: implies it). Level = 1 weak/low, 2 weak/high, 3 strong/low, 4 strong/high. Meet the seed's knob targets exactly (K, E, M, G and matched U).
- Each decisive-support item must establish its variant's finding from its own records plus the free evidence and, when it requires one, the subject account it names, without relying on another item. A deceptive item's records must tie the subject personally to the violation (not just show that something happened).
- Forbidden assertions (2-8 per variant): each is a specific false statement the subject is likely to make, and contradicts its named fact exactly as that fact is stated (never a broader claim).
- Free text never carries a protected fact: no FREE or PRIVATE ledger text, uncertainty entry of a FREE fact, persona background or matter states or implies a HOLD or CORE proposition of the same packet, alone or together with the other FREE texts. If FREE facts already establish a proposition, that proposition is FREE and carries no gate.
- Records, times, names and places agree with the canonical timeline everywhere (where each witness was at each time). A record that logs an event must be consistent with every other record of the same event.
- Opening question: open and neutral, from public material only. Three replay topics: noun phrases of at most 12 words, public material only; the replay script renders each topic into a question. The opening question and every rendered question must pass S2's relay test (relay_test.v1): 3 to 40 words; exactly one question mark, at the end; plain text (no angle brackets, braces, square brackets, backtick, vertical bar, asterisk, hash, underscore, tilde, URL or line break); no fact or record IDs; addressed to the subject directly (no "ask him", no "Question:" prefix); not compound (no second question joined by and/or); no tag question (", didn't you?"); no accusation, leading, demeanor or pressure wording; no case-specific claim the public packet does not hold.
- No role or verdict words and no IDs in any record text or public text (titles included). Never name the subject as the culprit in a record; records state evidence.
- S2 guard vocabulary (G3-G5: instructions to a model, role-play or prompt words, system or developer terms) never appears in any text.
- Content limits and the table rule (S1 6.1): a record's content is a plain-text body of at most 2,000 characters plus an optional table of at most 8 columns and 30 rows; every row has one cell per column; no column name or cell contains a vertical bar or a line break; the whole content object is at most 4,096 bytes as canonical JSON.
- Turn-label guide entries are labeled by content in S2 J1's order, never by role or style: the same answer to the same question gets the same label in both variants unless what the subject knows differs.
- Ledger rows and unknown-fact views are written in the second person ("You badged into ...").
