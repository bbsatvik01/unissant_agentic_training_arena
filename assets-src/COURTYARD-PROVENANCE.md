# Original Northstar Courtyard asset

The courtyard extension is original modeled geometry authored for the Arena commissioning user, who may use, modify and distribute it commercially. Existing Northstar precinct geometry is reused from its original editable source and retains its provenance. No source drawing, photograph, current museum floor plan, Smithsonian mark or external image texture is embedded in the asset.

Architectural reference: Old Patent Office Building, Washington, DC; Historic American Buildings Survey, HABS DC-130, sheets 1 and 3 of 8. Drawn by Hartman-Cox Architects. Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division. Drawing date not specified in the inspected record. Fictional training adaptation; not a representation of the building's current interior and not endorsed by the Smithsonian Institution.

The actual downloaded sheets were inspected. Sheet 1 describes four wings and an elongated courtyard; sheet 3 shows geometric planting, cross-axis paths and paired circular fountains. The new training composition uses paired planted circles, level paved routes and an 18 × 8 m central field. It is an original compact adaptation, not a measured scale replica. The documented building construction period, 1836–1867, is not the date of these later survey drawings.

- Official sheet 1: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/dc0194.sheet.00001a/
- Official sheet 3: https://www.loc.gov/resource/hhh.dc0194.sheet/?sp=3&st=image
- LOC HABS/HAER/HALS rights guidance: https://wwws.loc.gov/rr/print/res/114_habs.html
